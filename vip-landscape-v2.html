Our Process

From site walk
to long-term partnership.

01
Site Assessment

We walk the property with your team, documenting current conditions, identifying opportunities, and understanding your property's operational priorities and budget.

02
Custom Proposal

A detailed scope-of-work with itemized pricing, service frequency schedules, and enhancement recommendations — formatted for review by owners and asset managers.

03
Onboarding

We assign a dedicated account manager, establish communication protocols, and create a service calendar aligned with your property management workflow from day one.
